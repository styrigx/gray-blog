I recently scoured the web for Muse (Meta's personal AI assistant) tips — English and Chinese sources: X, Reddit, YouTube, V2EX, blogs, and reviews. Deduplicated, polished, and organized into ten categories: 501 tips total, now an open-source repo.

## Repo

👉 https://github.com/styrigx/muse-playbook

## Ten categories

- **Prompting** (53): how to talk to Muse so it gets it right the first time — delegation mindset, asking formulas, stop conditions for long tasks
- **Productivity** (62): reminders, email triage, docs and spreadsheets, meetings, creator ops, small-business use
- **Coding** (22): writing code, debugging, GitHub integration, PR review workflows
- **Learning** (14): languages, exam prep, micro-learning, tutor-style companionship
- **Writing** (23): newsletters, social posts, podcasts, video, editing and proofreading
- **Daily life** (110): saving money, travel, shopping comparisons, food and chores, social gifting — the breakout category
- **Research** (34): hands-on price comparisons, price tracking, sourcing, verification methods
- **Creative fun** (35): image prompt structures, podcasts, video, games, playful tricks
- **Hidden features** (108): Soul, memory, permissions and safety, chat operations, hardware, quota and invites
- **Advanced** (39): building connectors by talking, hooks automation, multi-agent setups, safety red lines
