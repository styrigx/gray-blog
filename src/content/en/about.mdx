## Who I am

Sloan Gray, known online as Styrigx.

Styrigx evolved from Sloan Gray. It carries the freedom and openness of Zelda, with traces of Gatsby, Nam-ra, and Lu Qiaochuan — romantic reinvention, calm responsibility, sincere bookishness. It's more of an identifier than a name; no need to pronounce it.

To understand the world, take it apart; to believe in it, put it back together.
